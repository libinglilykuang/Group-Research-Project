## Team Manifesto

1. Everyone should attend all meetings (unless informing the team leader otherwise).
2. All communication should be polite and professional and on Microsoft Teams for documentation.
3. We agree to follow pre-defined git branch and git commit naming conventions.
4. We agree to ask fellow team members for help and support if we are stuck or unsure what to do next.
5. We agree to provide detailed documentation regarding each stage of the project.
6. On weekdays, we agree to check teams and emails to keep ourselves informed of group activities and updates from industry.
7. We agree to leave any rooms we have used in a clean and tidy state once we leave.
8. We will try to complete all word before the set milestones to ensure everything is ready on time.
9. If any work is not ready on time we agree to inform the team leader in advance so that they are able to help and advise.
10. We agree as a team to make sure everyone is listened to and feel that they voice is heard.
11. We agree to raise any concerns we have quickly and to the right person 
12. We agree to have regular meetings and monitor attendance at each one.
13. We agree that all work will be our own and we will not committ academic misconduct by using chatGPT or other similar generative tools in our coursework.
14. We will conduct peer reviews honestly and fairly and in line with how each team member has contributed to the project.
15. We agree to recognise the risks involved with this project and ensure to plan to mitigate them.
