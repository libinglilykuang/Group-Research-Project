## Team Manifesto

1. Everyone should attend all meetings (unless informing the team leader otherwise) so that everyone is kept up to date with progress.
2. All communication should be polite and professional and we will provide detailed documentation on Microsoft Teams regarding each stage of the project for documentation and future reference.
3. We agree to follow pre-defined git branch and git commit naming conventions for uniformity and cohesion.
4. We agree to ask fellow team members for help and support if we are stuck or unsure what to do next to make sure we stay on schedule.
5. On weekdays, we agree to check teams and emails to keep ourselves informed of group activities and updates from industry.
6. We agree to leave any rooms we have used in a clean and tidy state once we leave.
7. We will try to complete all work before the set milestones to ensure everything is ready on time.
8. If any work is not ready on time we agree to inform the team leader in advance so that they are able to help and advise us so that we can stay organised and on schedule.
9. We agree as a team to make sure everyone is listened to and feel that they voice is heard in order to maintain harmony and keep the group motivated.
10. We agree to have regular meetings and make notes and monitor attendance at each one.
11. We agree that all work will be our own and we will not committ academic misconduct by using chatGPT or other similar generative tools in our coursework.
12. We will conduct peer reviews honestly and fairly and in line with how each team member has contributed to the project.
13. We agree to recognise the risks involved with this project and ensure to plan to mitigate them to ensure that we have a detailed plan from the start.
14. We agree to respect the team leader's decisions and if we have any issues or concerns to raise them politely and professionally to maintain an open discussion and so that all team members feel valued.
