## Team Manifesto

1. We agree as a team to make sure everyone is listened to and feel that they voice is heard, in order to maintain harmony and keep the group motivated.
2. Everyone should attend all meetings (unless informing the team leader otherwise) so that we are all involved in project discussions.
3. All communication should be polite, professional and clear as this keeps information organised and able to be referenced in future when needed.
4. We agree to follow test driven development so that we can plan how our project will function from the beginning, giving us a clear end goal to work towards.
5. On weekdays, we agree to check Microsoft Teams and our university emails to keep ourselves informed of and updates from industry or fellow team members.
6. If any work is not likely to be ready on time we agree to inform the team leader so that they are able to help and advise how we can mitigate potential issues.
7. We agree to have regular meetings and make notes and monitor attendance at each one so every team member stays informed across all aspects of the project.
8. We agree that all work will be our own i.e no use of AI or other generative tools, to promote our own technical growth and development and to produce work we are proud of.
9. We agree to respect the team leader's decisions and if we have any issues or concerns to raise them politely and professionally to maintain an open discussion and to ensure all team members feel valued whilst still following the best solution.
10. We agree to ask fellow team members for support if we are unsure what to do next so we stay on schedule.
