## Team Manifesto

1. Everyone should attend all meetings (unless informing the team leader otherwise) so that we are all involved in project discussions.
2. All communication should be polite and professional and on Microsoft Teams. This allows for clear and organised information which can be referenced in future when needed.
3. We agree to ask fellow team members for help and support if we are stuck or unsure what to do next to make sure we stay on schedule.
4. On weekdays, we agree to check teams and emails to keep ourselves informed of group activities and updates from industry.
5. If any work is not ready on time we agree to inform the team leader in advance so that they are able to help and advise us so that we can stay organised and on schedule.
6. We agree as a team to make sure everyone is listened to and feel that they voice is heard in order to maintain harmony and keep the group motivated.
7. We agree to have regular meetings and make notes and monitor attendance at each one so every team member to stay informed across all aspects of the project.
8. We agree that all work will be our own i.e no use of AI or other generative tools to promote our own technical growth and development and to produce work we are proud of.
9. We agree to follow test driven development so that we can plan how our project will function from the beginning, giving us a clear end goal to work towards.
10. We agree to respect the team leader's decisions and if we have any issues or concerns to raise them politely and professionally to maintain an open discussion and so that all team members feel valued whilst still following the best solution.
