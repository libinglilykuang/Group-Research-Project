## Team Manifesto

1.	All communication should be polite, professional and clear as this keeps information organised and able to be referenced in future when needed. – forming
2.	We will ensure that our skills, strengths, weaknesses and preferences are properly communicated so that work can be delegated and executed effectively. – forming
3.	We agree to have regular meetings, make notes, monitor attendance and ensure every individual is listened to so that we are all involved and informed across all aspects of the project. - storming
4.	We agree to respect the team leader's decisions - if we have any issues or disagreements, we will raise them politely and professionally to maintain an open discussion and to ensure all team members feel valued. – storming
5.	We agree to follow test driven development so that we can plan how our project will function from the beginning, giving us a clear end goal to work towards. - norming
6.	We know that everyone must contribute to the project for its success, and so will all strive to have responsibility and ambition towards completing our work. - norming 
7.	When agreeing on a mutual plan for our work process, we understand that we may need to compromise on our ideas for the functioning of the team. - norming
8.	We will utilise Agile methodologies to assign tasks fairly to team members, allowing individuals to carry out work competently and autonomously. - performing
9.	If any work is not likely to be ready on time, we agree to inform the team leader so that they are able to help and advise how we can mitigate potential issues. - performing
10.	We agree to check Microsoft Teams and our university emails to keep ourselves informed of updates from industry or team members, and to ask the team for support if we are unsure what to do, to stay on schedule. - performing
